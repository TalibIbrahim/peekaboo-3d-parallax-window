Peekaboo

Your screen becomes a window. Your webcam follows your head, and the 3D world behind the glass shifts exactly as a real one would.

Video is processed on your device and never leaves this page. For the best effect, go fullscreen and sit about an arm's length away.

  • Close one eye. The illusion works best for a single eye: with both open, your brain sees the screen is flat.
  • Move slowly side to side and toward the screen. Things behind the glass should stay "put" in the room.
  • Set your screen size in Settings (use Measure… if unsure). A wrong size makes depth feel off.
  • Good, even lighting on your face makes tracking much steadier.
  • In mouse mode, move the pointer to "lean" and scroll to move closer or further.
Measure your screen

Hold a bank card (or any standard ID card) flat against the screen and resize the outline until it matches the card exactly.
